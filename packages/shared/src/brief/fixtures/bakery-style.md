# Style: מאפיית הצפון
From your files: brand-guide.pdf, logo-north-bakery.png, reference-reel.mp4.

## Look
- Colours: Oven brown #7A3E1D (titles, logo), Flour cream #F3E2C0 (backgrounds, captions)
- Fonts: Heebo 800 for titles and captions (brand-guide.pdf)
- Titles: centred, cream on a brown band, in the lower third of the frame
- Lower thirds: name and role, bottom left, cream on brown
- Captions: Heebo 800, cream with a brown outline, two lines at most
- Logo: logo-north-bakery.png, bottom right on the end card

## Editing
- Format: 1080×1920, 30 s at most
- Pacing: fast, cuts on the music’s beat
- Graphics: light; a title, a lower third and an end card
- Music: warm acoustic tracks the client picks
- Sound effects: none

## Do
- Open on the oven or the counter, never on a title
- End on the logo with the opening hours

## Don’t
- Show prices
- Use stock footage

## Kit
- kit/title-card: the title on a brown band
- kit/lower-third: name and role

## Reference
- reference-reel.mp4: match the title size and the quick cuts
