- Getting the song, when the brief asks for one (above). The script finds the song, downloads it, cuts the part the video uses and puts it at edit/preview/music.mp3 itself: never search for, download, convert or trim a song any other way (no yt-dlp, no curl, no ffmpeg), and never read or print a key. Once the cut's shape is fixed (you know the time of every cut), and before you build the composition, write edit/music/plan.json from that shape exactly as "Making it" below says: its lengthMs is the composition's exact duration, which is the length of the part the song command cuts, and if the song fails the same plan is what make uses. Then run the song command as a Bash command of its own, in the foreground, with a Bash timeout of 600000 ms (it can take minutes):
  - A song: `node "{{musicScript}}" song "<edit dir>" --title "<title>" --artist "<artist>" [--version "<version>"] [--link "<link>"] --from <screenshot|link|name> [--file "<the screenshot>"] --by brief`. Take the words from the brief's music.song exactly as they are, in their own language, and leave out each option whose value is null. --from is screenshot when the song was read off a screenshot (--file is its name, song.fromFile), link when a link is all there is (then give --link alone, without --title and --artist), and name when the song was named in words.
  - A trending song: `node "{{musicScript}}" song "<edit dir>" --trending --from trending --by editor`. The niche comes from the client's style when its Client style section has a "Reels niche:" line. Without that line, add `--niche "#a #b #c"`: 2 to 4 hashtags for what the video shows, for example #fpv #fpvdrone for a drone video.
  - Where the words come in. When the brief says the song opens on music alone and its words start at a moment of the picture (music.note, the instructions or the client's style, for example "starts without lyrics; the lyrics start on the beat when the image opens"), add `--lyrics-at <ms>` to the song call above: that moment's time in the cut, in ms (you know it, the cut's shape is fixed). The script finds the song's first word and cuts the part to start that long before it, so the song's own intro is the lead-in and the first word is heard at that moment. Play edit/preview/music.mp3 from the cut's first frame (data-start 0), and never delay or silence its start to keep the words back. The line's `lyricsMs` is where the first word is heard in the part: if it is smaller than the ms you asked, the song has a shorter intro, so start music.mp3 that much later in the cut and say so in project.md. With this option the line's `dropMs` is null: put the cut at that moment on the beat nearest `lyricsMs` when that beat is within 80 ms of it, else leave it where it is, and move the other cuts onto the nearest `beats`. Where the beats wander under the singing, count whole beats at the line's `bpm` from the nearest of its `downbeats`.
  - Where the part starts. When the brief names a moment of the song to start from (music.note, the instructions or the client's style, for example "start the song at 0:37"), add `--start-ms <ms>` to the song call above: that moment in the song, in ms (0:37 is 37000). The part starts exactly there, so the song plays from that moment; play edit/preview/music.mp3 from the cut's first frame (data-start 0). Never give it with `--lyrics-at`. With this option the line's `dropMs` and `lyricsMs` are null: move the cuts onto the nearest `beats`, and where the beats wander under the singing, count whole beats at the line's `bpm` from the nearest of its `downbeats`. If the line's note says the part starts earlier than asked (the song ends too soon after that moment), say so in project.md.
  - On {"ok":true,…}: edit/preview/music.mp3 is now the song's part, at the plan's length and −20 LUFS like every version. The line gives `part` ({startMs, endMs, how}: where in the song the part starts and ends, and how it was found) and `beats` (the part's beats, in ms from its start). Fit the cut to the part exactly as to a supplied track, as "Fitting the cut to it" below says: move the cuts onto the nearest beats (use the line's `beats`; about one frame early reads as on the beat) and the drop's cut onto the part's drop (find it by measuring edit/preview/music.mp3 as "Fitting the cut to it" says), keeping the total length, and write in project.md what you moved. Skip the drop check there: never make music to move a song's drop. If part.how is "whole" the song is shorter than the cut: loop it on a bar line of its beats, or let it end with a ramp-out, and say which in project.md. Songs have words: under speech duck the song 12 to 15 dB below its music-only level as for any music, and where it sings over someone talking duck it harder, or put the speech over an instrumental stretch of the part, and say which in project.md.
  - On {"ok":false,"code":…,"message":…} with a non-zero exit, the script has recorded why and the music is as it was. Say why in project.md's Assumptions line, in one plain sentence from its message, then make the music as in "Making it" below: a song was asked for, so a made track is still the stated wish (still at most two makes), and don't run the song command again for that song in this run.
  - If edit/music/music.json already shows the song from an earlier run of this job ("source": "song") and the cut's shape has not changed, keep it and don't get it again.
