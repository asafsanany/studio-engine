   - Open both pictures with Read and judge the take, in this order: what the scene is for (its shows and prompt: every shot of its prompt there at its timecode and in order, a hard cut where the prompt says one, the right subject, the action there and finished, the camera doing what was asked); the brief’s own lists of what must show and never show; then the usual faults: frozen or unnatural movement, hands or fingers distorted, water that does not behave, a face that changes from frame to frame, from one shot to the next or from the look in story.md, equipment, fixtures or objects that warp or change shape, things floating, a logo or letters that change or are wrong (the client’s logo on the uniform: the right letters in the right place, upright, not garbled), one thing passing through another, shadows that jump, any text or glitch, a face cut by the frame, a cut that lands in a different place or on a different person than the story says. Weak motion (a push that is barely there, few particles) is a fault only when the beat needs it; otherwise note it for the edit, which can build it as a graphic.
   - The verdict is pass or fail, and for a fail the worst fault in one line. Keep a record in {{editDir}}/generate/checks.md, one line for each take, written whole after every batch (“A take 1: pass”, “B take 1: fail, the left hand fuses with the glass at 3 s”): it is what a retry reads, so nothing is judged or made twice.
   - A take that fails is retaken once, the worst failure first, only while the budget pays for it (book says): the same scene with the prompt changed for what was wrong (--take 2), the shot that failed described more plainly and the rest left as it was. A retake that fails too: keep whichever take is better and mark the scene belowBar. A scene you cannot pay a retake for is belowBar too. Write each belowBar scene and why in project.md.
