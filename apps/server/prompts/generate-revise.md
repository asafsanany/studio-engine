- Generated footage: this video has no footage. Its clips and its voice were made for it and are in edit/generate/ (shots.json, voice-words.json, plan.json, voice/, clips/, footage.mp4, sfx/, spend.json); edit/brief.md lists them. Read edit/generate/shots.json and edit/generate/voice-words.json with the notes, and sort each note into one of three kinds:
  - (a) An edit change (a cut, the words or look of a caption, timing, music, a graphic, a size or a position): do it as for any footage, in the composition. It costs nothing.
  - (b) What is in a clip (a hand, a face, the logo, a bent pole, the water, a thing that melts or passes through another): make ONE new take of that scene (a clip), with its prompt from edit/generate/plan.json changed for what is wrong and the rest of the prompt left as it was. Two notes about one clip are one take. Never more than one new take of a clip in a round.
  - (c) The voice (how a line is said: "stress the BUT more", a pause, slower, louder): make a new take of that line only, as described below.
- Money. The Higgsfield budget for this whole video is {{generateBudget}}, every round included. {{generateSpent}} is spent and {{generateLeft}} is left; edit/generate/spend.json (the entries that are not refunded) is the truth, so read it before the first take. A new take costs about what the kept take cost (its usd in shots.json). Count the new takes the notes need, put the worst failure first (a face or a hand that breaks the picture, then the logo and equipment that change shape, then the water and the small things), and make as many as {{generateLeft}} pays for. A book call the budget cannot pay for exits 2 with the code over_budget and books nothing, so you do not make the take: that is the end of new takes for this round, not an error. For every note the money could not cover, change nothing about its clip and say so in its line of the round ("not made: the budget is spent"), and name the clips you would retake with more budget in project.md. The voice is ElevenLabs and does not count against it.
- A new take of a scene (a clip) is made through the Higgsfield connector, on the plan’s credits, and booked with the script at {{generateShotsScript}}. Load the connector’s tools first with ToolSearch, query `select:mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield__generate_video,mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield__jobs_wait,mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield__media_upload,mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield__media_confirm,mcp__claude_ai_Higgsfield__models_explore`; no other tool of the connector is allowed, never ask for free or unlimited generations (use_unlim false), never pass a folder_id, and make no picture of any kind. Each script call takes the edit folder "{{generateEditDir}}" in double quotes, runs as a Bash command of its own and prints one JSON line. Put text in single quotes and write an apostrophe inside it as ’. The five steps, one tool call each:
  - Price it, free (the answer’s credits_exact is the price to book; a notice that suggests a preset is never used: call again with declined_preset_id set to the preset id in the notice, and send it on the real call too): generate_video with get_cost true and the parameters you will send: model kling4_0, mode text-to-video (reference-to-video with the logo as image_references for a scene that shows the person in the uniform, as plan.json’s references say), resolution 720p, duration the scene’s seconds from plan.json, aspect_ratio the main format’s, and the scene’s prompt from plan.json with what must be different said plainly in the shot that failed and the rest left as it was.
  - Book it: `node "{{generateShotsScript}}" book "{{generateEditDir}}" <id> --kind clip --model kling4_0 --credits <the price> --budget {{generateBudgetNumber}} --take <N> --seconds <n>`. N is the next free take number of that scene (`ls "{{generateEditDir}}/generate/clips/<id>"`), and --budget is always the whole video’s budget, {{generateBudgetNumber}}.
  - Make it: the same generate_video call without get_cost. It answers a job id: book again with --job <the job id> added.
  - Wait: jobs_wait with the job id until all_terminal (call it again after poll_after_seconds). A scene takes about four minutes.
  - Save it: `node "{{generateShotsScript}}" save "{{generateEditDir}}" <id> --kind clip --take <N> --url '<the result url>' --job <the job id>` (it takes the soundtrack out). A generation that failed, was blocked or canceled charged nothing: `node "{{generateShotsScript}}" void "{{generateEditDir}}" <id> --kind clip --take <N> --reason '<why>'`, and say it in the note’s line. If the connector says the plan has no credits left, make nothing more: that is the end of new takes for this round, said in the notes’ lines.
- Check the new take as the Generate step does: frames at its start, 25 %, 50 %, 75 % and end with ffmpeg, looked at for hands and fingers, water, faces, the logo, equipment changing shape, objects passing through each other and freezing. Keep the better of the old and the new take. If the new one still fails and the budget is spent, keep the better one and mark that shot belowBar. Then swap the kept take in: in edit/generate/shots.json change only that shot's file, take, usd, seconds and belowBar (everything else, the order included, stays as it is), copy the kept file over edit/generate/shots/<id>.mp4, and re-cut that clip only: its windows are unchanged, so take its ranges from the new file and bake the cut again (and, on a job with more than one format, make each other format's baked cut from it, as the formats part of the finish says). Never touch another clip.
- A note about the voice: find the line spoken at the note's time in edit/generate/voice-words.json (its `lines` give each line's id, take, startMs and endMs). Then, one Bash call each:
  - `node "{{generateVoiceScript}}" line "{{generateEditDir}}" --id <L#> --text '<the line, word for word as plan.json has it, numbers spelled as words as before>' --direction '<its old direction with what the note asks said plainly, such as "stress BUT more">'` (it makes the next free take and prints its number);
  - `node "{{generateVoiceScript}}" check "{{generateEditDir}}" --file voice/<L#>-take<N>.mp3 --text '<the same text>'` with the take the line call printed: its `match` must be true; if not, make one more take (three at most in all) and keep the best;
  - `node "{{generateVoiceScript}}" assemble "{{generateEditDir}}" --order L1:<take>,L2:<take>,… --pauses "L1>L2:<ms>,…" --lead <leadMs>` with the take every line has now in voice-words.json's `lines` (the new take for the remade line) and, for every pair of neighbouring lines, the pause they had (the next line's startMs minus the previous line's endMs), so no other line changes. The `--lead` is the `leadMs` that voice-words.json records for the opening before the first word (leave `--lead` out when it has none), so the opening is kept. The first line's startMs is that opening, not a pause: never count it as one.
  - The voice now has a new length: every word after that line moves by the difference. Shift the cut by it, never stretching the voice: the shot under the line is trimmed or held to the new length, every shot after it and everything timed to the voice after it (captions, cards, sound effect cues, the end card, the music's lane) moves by the same amount, and the windows in shots.json are updated to the new times (voice-words.json is already new). Write edit/transcripts/voice.json again from it.

Both scripts read their own keys: never open a .env file and never print or pass a key.
