# Difficulty index for review notes

Sort one of the owner's review notes into one of three kinds. The kind decides how the note is done: a quick fix happens at once in the live preview's composition; the other two go to the editor when they send their notes.

## quick: the layer on top of the cut
Only the composition's own text and graphics change. The cut (cut.mp4), the shots, the grade, the music and the sound stay exactly as they are.
- Fonts and weights of captions, titles or any added text ("change the font to Heebo Bold", "thinner", "@Heebo Bold").
- The words of a caption or a title: spelling, a word or a line added, changed or removed.
- Colours, sizes, positions and backgrounds of captions, titles and graphics ("higher", "bigger", "yellow", "keep the size").
- When a caption, a title or a graphic comes in or goes out, and how it moves, as long as the cut stays the same.
- Removing a caption, a title, a line or another added element. Adding a title, a logo or a library graphic (@graphics/...) that is already in the job's files.
- Matching the text style of another moment of the same video.
The owner's notes of this kind: change font (8), replace subtitle text (4), remove subtitle text (3), subtitle position, change text color, thinner text weight, keep text size, match text style, remove title, remove red line, add end logo.

## revise: one part of the cut, or one baked step
- Swap, trim, move or remove a shot; change the opening shot; remove a pan.
- The look of the footage, in one part or all of it: exposure, colour filters, a LUT (@luts/...). The grade is baked into the cut.
- The music (another track, where it starts, louder or softer) or a sound effect.
The owner's notes of this kind: replace shot (7), trim shot start, change opening shot, remove fast pans, fix overexposed shots, remove color filters, replace music, add typing sound.

## full: the whole video, or taste across all of it
- Pacing, rhythm, cuts on the beat, the length of the video.
- The choice or the order of shots across the video, continuity.
- The format (portrait or landscape), reframing, stabilising.
- The grade style of the whole video.
- A new custom animation or graphic that isn't in the library.
- Something is broken ("the video doesn't play", "it's black").
The owner's notes of this kind: improve shot selection (2), sync cuts to beat (2), google search style (2), improve shot continuity, shorten video, portrait video format, stabilize drone shots, color grading style, video not working.

## Rules
1. When unsure between two kinds, take the bigger one.
2. Text that is part of the footage itself (a sign, a shirt, burned-in text) is not quick: it is at least revise.
3. A note about what the picture shows ("this shot", "the sky is too bright", "her face") is at least revise.
4. A note with several asks takes the biggest kind among them.
5. Notes can be in Hebrew, in English, or both.
6. The composition notes from project.md tell an added caption or title apart from text in the footage.
7. A note that asks to cut on the beat or to sync the cuts to the music, even with a complaint that one part drags ("cut on the beat", "the middle drags, put the cuts on the beat"), is revise, not full, when "About the cut" below says the cut is already on the beat: it moves a few cuts and does not re-cut the video. Without an "About the cut" part, or when it says the cut is not on the beat, the note stays full.
