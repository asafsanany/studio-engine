Generated footage: this video has no footage. Its clips, its voice and its sound effects were made for it by Studio Engine's Generate step and are in ./edit/generate/ (the "Generated footage" section of edit/brief.md lists them). Cut by these rules: where they and the skill's defaults disagree, these win.
- The footage is the clips of edit/generate/shots.json, in its order, as edit/generate/shots/<id>.mp4 (the brief's file list has no footage file; these clips are what you cut). They are silent and made at 720p (720x1280 for a 9:16 video) at 24 fps, since the frame rate depends on the model that made the clip, so scale them to the canvas and conform them to the composition's frame rate. Each clip is a whole scene of 3 to 30 seconds with several shots already cut together at the timecodes of its prompt (edit/generate/story.md tells the scene in words, and edit/generate/footage.mp4, when it is there, is the scenes joined in order): cut a scene as one piece, keep its inner cuts and do not add a transition inside it. Their soundtrack was taken out. A shot's windows are the stretches of the voice it covers, in seconds from the start of voice.mp3. A clip with several windows is cut in once for each, each time from where its last use stopped (never the same moment twice in a row, never a loop). Fit a clip to a window by trimming it or by holding its last frame for up to half a second, and never by changing its speed by more than 10 %. If a clip falls short of its windows by more than that, end the window on a slow push-in on its last frame (a HyperFrames layer) and say so in project.md.
- The speech is edit/generate/voice.mp3 and its word times are in edit/generate/voice-words.json: every spoken word with its line (L1, L2, …), startSec and endSec. Do not transcribe it again and do not run the transcribe helper on it: write edit/transcripts/voice.json from voice-words.json as {"words":[{"type":"word","text":"…","start":0.0,"end":0.4}]} (seconds) so the packing step, the Transcribe row and the captions all read one transcript, and carry on from there. Time 0 of voice.mp3 is time 0 of the cut: windows, word times and the owner's review notes all use that one timeline, so move nothing of it. voice.mp3 may open with silence: leadMs in edit/generate/voice-words.json (missing means 0) is how long, and the word times already include it. That opening, from 0 to the first word, is where the opening clips and the alert sound effect go, and nothing more is added before the voice. It is the cut's only speech: play it as it is and never stretch, speed up, trim, re-time or re-encode it. The picture follows the voice, never the other way round; if the clips are silent, give the baked cut the voice as its audio (if render.py will not take silent sources, make the cut's picture with ffmpeg and put voice.mp3 under it).
- Cut every clip to the voice. A shot changes on the first word of its beat or in the pause just before it, never in the middle of a word, and the payoff of a graphic lands on its spoken word. Never drop, merge or reorder a story beat of the voiceover.
- Captions are the script's words exactly as the brief's voiceover lines spell them (numbers, symbols and punctuation too), timed from voice-words.json: a caption shows from the first spoken word of its group to the last. Build edit/animations/words_out.json from them (text, start and end in seconds on the cut) without mapping through EDL ranges, and build the captions as a HyperFrames layer (§3.4). Their look is the one recorded in edit/generate/reference.md and the brief, in the client's brand colours (for example white bold condensed capitals in a dark navy rounded box with a thin blue edge, key words in the brand blue). A caption never sits over a face, the equipment or the object a shot is about, and a scene's text is gone before the next scene's comes in.
- Graphics are HyperFrames, never part of a clip and never asked of the model: the offer cards, badges, an alert pill and its edge glow, particles the clip did not time, the end card. Use the client's logo file as it is, cut out of its background if it only comes on one, with no box behind it and no line through its words. Anything that must land on a sound (an alert pulse on the alert hits) starts on the start of that sound effect's file. The end card is fully built (the logo, its headline, the offer and the button, as the brief lists them) and held for at least 3 seconds; if the voice ends sooner, the video runs on past its last word. Whatever the brief's must-avoid list names (a phone number, a claim) appears nowhere, on a card, in a caption or on the end card.
- Sound: the voice sits above the music. Duck the music 12 to 15 dB under the voice, bring it back up in the gaps and over the end card, and keep the voice itself at the level it has. The sound effects in edit/generate/sfx/ go at the cues the brief and reference.md give (measure each file with ffprobe and put its start on the moment it belongs to), well under the voice. The music is made to the cut as for any job, and since the voice is speech it should be sparse under it.
- A shot marked belowBar in shots.json (its best take failed the Generate step's check and no better one could be paid for) is used as it is: do not try to fix, regenerate or hide it, and list it in project.md under Assumptions ("Below the bar: clip B, what is wrong with it") so the owner sees it in Review.0 to about 1.08, toward the part that matters.
- A clip file that is missing is not cut: cut around it and say so in Assumptions. Never change, move or delete anything in edit/generate/ and never run its scripts: a new take of a clip or of a voice line is made only in a revise round.
