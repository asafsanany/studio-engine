- Camera log: the colour grade never guesses a camera LUT, and log footage has to have one. The wrapper's report.json says what each source measures as (footage.kind): log, display, or ambiguous (log or a very flat display grade: it cannot tell, and real log often lands here), and lists a blocked source that has no camera_lut under needs_camera_lut. A camera LUT converts one camera's log footage, so never set one on a source the report calls display, and never choose one for its look. The one exception is a source the report lists under weak_log: it measured log or ambiguous on only three of the four log signs (real log shows all four; a calm sky can show three), so the wrapper graded it as display and did not block. Keep it display and say so in project.md, unless its files name a log curve in the lines below: then handle it as log.
