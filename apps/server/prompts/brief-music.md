- Music. Studio Engine makes music only when the job asks for it. music.wanted says whether the instructions ask for music to be made or added to this video: true or false, never left out.
  - True only when the owner’s note, a voice note, a chat message or a document asks for music in so many words: “add music”, “with a soundtrack”, “something energetic under it”, “intense music with drops”, “תוסיפו מוזיקה”, “עם מוזיקה”. Then music is an object (file null) and music.note keeps only what the instructions say about how it should sound (“intense, with drops”), or null.
  - False in every other case, and music stays null when the instructions say nothing about music. Never true because music would fit this video, its tone, its platform or the footage. A reference video that has music in it is a style to match, not an ask for music. A line that only says what music the client likes (“they like lo-fi”) is not an ask, and neither is a form or template whose music part is left empty (“מוזיקה:” with nothing written).
  - False too when the instructions name a music file (music.file, role music: the editor uses that file) or a song (the song is the ask), and when they say no music (music.note “no music”). Conflicts follow step 4: The owner’s note first, then the newest message.
